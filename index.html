    Timezone

      Daylight saving, in local time: when the clock jumps forward, the minutes it skips are not listed. When the clock goes back, a minute that happens twice is listed once. These are the times a wall clock shows. Another cron may choose differently.

      Share

      Five-field Vixie/POSIX cron only. Seconds fields, year fields and Quartz tokens (?, L, W, #) are rejected by name. Assertions live in tests.html.
